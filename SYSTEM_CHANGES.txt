1. ZERO HARDCODED MOCKS & 100% REAL FIRESTORE FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real-time Database Collections:
  * `complaints`: Scoped queries (`onSnapshot`) with role-based filtering:
    - `CITIZEN`: Filtered to grievances filed by current user (`citizenUid == auth.currentUser.uid` or reporter match).
    - `FIELD_CREW`: Filtered to grievances in crew's assigned ward (`assignedWard == userProfile.assignedWard`).
    - `WARD_OFFICER`: Filtered strictly to complaints in `userProfile.assignedWard`.
    - `SUPER_ADMIN`: Unrestricted apex access across all wards and national municipal jurisdictions.
  * `units`: Live municipal crew and repair vehicle fleet records:
    - Real-time `onSnapshot` tracking of crew location, status (`AVAILABLE`, `EN_ROUTE`, `MAINTENANCE`), equipment, and active assignments.
  * `users`: User profiles with hierarchical RBAC roles: `CITIZEN`, `FIELD_CREW`, `WARD_OFFICER`, `SUPER_ADMIN`.
- Firestore Writes:
  * Citizen Grievance Submission: Creates records with `serverTimestamp()`, AI Vision diagnostics, and geospatial coordinates.
  * Officer Crew Dispatch & Work Order Allocation: Atomic updates to `complaints` and `units`.
  * Status Transitions & Proof-of-Fix: Uploads remediation photo URLs, inspector closure notes, and resolution timestamps.
